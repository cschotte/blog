## About Me

Clemens Schotte is a Dutch technology leader, software architect, and entrepreneur with 25+ years of experience building and delivering software products and platforms. After a long career at Microsoft, including leadership roles around Azure Maps, he now focuses on helping organizations improve processes through cloud technology, AI, and automation while continuing to pursue personal technology projects such as modernizing his classic game CaveRace in Zig. He combines deep engineering expertise with product and program leadership, and has a strong interest in practical, high-quality software engineering.
