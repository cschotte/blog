# Third-party assets

This site self-hosts a small number of third-party assets instead of loading them from a CDN.

## Roboto

- Files: `static/fonts/roboto-variable.woff2`, `static/fonts/roboto-variable-italic.woff2`
- License: Apache License, Version 2.0 — https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
- Source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Roboto

## KaTeX

- Files: `static/katex/`
- License: MIT — https://github.com/KaTeX/KaTeX/blob/main/LICENSE
- Source: https://katex.org/
- Loaded only on pages with `math: true` in front matter.
